GoatMediaStackarr

A self-hosted media stack in one container: request, download and watch films and TV on your own server.

GoatMediaStackarr runs on your own computer or server. It is free, open-source software, not an online service: there is no account with us, and nothing you do in it is sent to us.

It can copy its backups to your own Google Drive. When you connect Google Drive, Google asks whether GoatMediaStackarr may use the files it creates in your Drive; it cannot see any of your other files.
