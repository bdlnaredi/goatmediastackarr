Privacy policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

GoatMediaStackarr is free, open-source software that you install and run on your own server. It is not an online service: whoever runs it is in charge of it and of the data on it. The people who make GoatMediaStackarr do not run a server for it and receive no data from it.

Google Drive

You can choose to connect Google Drive so that GoatMediaStackarr copies its backups there. If you do:

  • What it can reach. It asks Google for access to only the files and folders it creates in your Drive (the drive.file permission), and for your email address, to show which account is connected. It cannot see, open or change any other file in your Drive.
  • What it does with it. It uploads backups of its own settings to a folder in your Drive, lists that folder, and deletes its oldest backups there so only the number you chose is kept. Nothing else.
  • Where it is kept. The access Google gives (a sign-in token) and your email address are stored only on your own server, in GoatMediaStackarr's settings. They are not sent to anyone else.
  • No sharing, no selling. Data from Google is not sold, shared, used for advertising, or used to train any AI model.
  • Stopping. Press Disconnect in Settings → Backup to delete the token from your server. You can also remove GoatMediaStackarr's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Backups already in your Drive stay there until you delete them.

GoatMediaStackarr's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The sign-in forwarding page

After you allow access, Google sends your browser to a page on this site, which passes it straight on to your own GoatMediaStackarr. That page runs in your browser and stores and sends nothing. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep standard web server logs (see GitHub's privacy statement).
